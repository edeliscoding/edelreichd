About

I work as a Drupal developer on the Auraria Library website, where I build and maintain custom themes, modules, and integrations that serve a shared academic campus. My background began in IT support at the Department of Anesthesiology at the University of Colorado Denver, where I took on development of intranet and public-facing websites and internal web applications — the starting point for my move into full-time web development.

Alongside Drupal, I build SaaS applications and React/Next.js front ends, working across the stack from database design and REST/JSON:API integrations to accessible, responsive interfaces. I care about solutions that hold up in production, not just demos.

  • SaaS application development
  • React and Next.js front-end development
  • REST and JSON:API integrations
  • Web accessibility
